# Enemies

Monsters are organized by level ranges. Each range contains 2-3 monsters of increasing difficulty and experience reward. Higher difficulty mobs teach the player new combat mechanics (dodging, timing, kiting, etc.).

Stat descriptions are relative to the monster's level range:
- **low** = significantly below average for the range
- **high** = significantly above average for the range
- **bad** = worse than default (e.g. bad cooldown = slower recovery between attacks)

## Range 1 - Level 1~2

### Piou
- **Level:** 1
- **Difficulty:** Low
- **Locations:** Tulimshar
- **Behaviour:** Flee
- **Stats:** low atk, low hp

### Maggot
- **Level:** 1
- **Difficulty:** Low
- **Locations:** Tulimshar, Sandstorm
- **Behaviour:** Flee
- **Stats:** low atk, fast atk speed

### Peyote
- **Level:** 2
- **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Locations:** Everywhere across Tonori (outdoor)
- **Behaviour:** Immobile
- **Stats:** low atk speed, high atk
